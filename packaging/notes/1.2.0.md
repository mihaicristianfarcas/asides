**Search your asides.** In History, `⌘F` (`Ctrl+F` on Windows and Linux) lists the asides that say every word you type, in any order, ignoring case and accents: in what you said, the pins, the app and page, and the session each went to. `asides history` followed by words, and the agents' `list_asides`, search the same way.

**Copy an aside with its screenshots.** A copied aside now pastes in one go into a mail, a note or a chat, each screenshot where you took it; a terminal still gets the text, with the screenshots' paths. On Windows and Linux the screenshots come inside the HTML. Turn on Copy each aside (General, or the menu) to have each one copied once it's sent or saved.

**Updates are checked more closely.** On a Mac, an update whose zip names a file two different ways is refused.
