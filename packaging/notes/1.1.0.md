**asides runs on Windows.** Windows 10 (2004) or 11, x64 or Arm64. It types into agents in Windows Terminal, VS Code's terminal or any console, and opens new sessions in Windows Terminal. Only the microphone needs a permission. The installer needs no administrator, and asides updates itself when a new version comes out.

**asides runs on Linux.** GNOME or KDE on Wayland, or any X11 desktop, x86_64 or Arm64, with Vulkan. tmux, Konsole and WezTerm take the text themselves, and kitty does with remote control on; other terminals go through a small helper, which on Ubuntu and Arch you allow once in setup. asides says when a new version is out, for your package manager to install.

**Measuring no longer stops at grain.** On a grainy picture, such as a photo or a noisy wallpaper, specks of gray grain could count as edges, and measuring stopped every few pixels. A faint edge now has to go on all along its contour.

**Every download is signed.** The Mac's is notarized by Apple, Windows' is signed by its publisher, and each file has a minisign signature beside it. asides.mihaicristian.dev picks the download for your system.

- Mac: `brew upgrade --cask asides` if you have it, or download `asides-1.1.0.dmg` and drag asides to Applications. Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later.
- Windows: run the installer for your processor (x64 or Arm64).
- Linux: install the `.deb` or `.rpm` for your processor, or unpack the `.tar.gz` and run its `install.sh`.
