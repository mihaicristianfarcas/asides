**Record clips.** Press `K`, or the pill's camera, to record what happens next on the live screen. The aside gets the moments the app in front changed, numbered and timed: each screen it settled on, a frame of each movement between them, and anything that flashed for an instant. A pin's camera, or `⌘K` in its card, records only that pin's part of the screen.

**Follow up on the last aside.** Press `R` and the last aside's pins show again where they were, hollow, with their drawings and moves. Click one's number to pin what's there now: the new pin says which one it follows up on, so your agent can compare. `R` again hides the pins you didn't follow up on.

**Agents can look again.** For 15 minutes after an aside, your agent can see what a pin was on as it is now, to check its change: `asides look`, or the `look_again` tool. It sees only the window the pin was in, never while asides is open, and the menu bar shows an eye each time. General → Let agents look again for changes how long, or turns it off.

**22 agents.** Besides Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Gemini CLI, Copilot CLI and Qwen Code, asides now types into Cursor CLI, Droid, Amp, Kiro CLI, goose, Crush, Cline, Kilo Code, Auggie, Pi, Continue CLI, iFlow CLI, Mistral Vibe, Kimi Code, Letta Code and Aider.

**More terminals.** On a Mac, iTerm, WezTerm and kitty, where new sessions open too. Anywhere, a pane of tmux, Zellij or GNU screen, or Neovim's terminal: an agent in Warp, VS Code, Zed or any other app gets asides when it runs in one of them. On Windows, agents in WSL too.

**Redo.** `⇧⌘Z` (`Ctrl+Shift+Z` on Windows and Linux) puts back what Undo took away, step by step.

**Smaller changes.** What asides tells you, and a button's name, now show by the pill on the live screen too. In an aside of several freezes, pins number on from the last freeze instead of starting at 1 again. In the asides window, the sidebar and History's list can be dragged wider or narrower, the window opens where you left it, and text too long for its place ends with an ellipsis instead of stopping mid-letter. On a Mac, the menu bar's History… takes `⌘1`. Help plays each demonstration with asides' own pieces, and has a pill to try.
