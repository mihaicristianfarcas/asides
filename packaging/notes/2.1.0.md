**A tooltip for a pin's clip.** While a pin's camera (or `⌘K` in its card) records its part of the screen, a tooltip beside it shows how long, and takes what happens there: type, or talk with its microphone, and it goes in the pin. Its buttons end the clip and stay live, end it and freeze the screen again, or throw it away and put you back on the frozen screen as it was.

**Notes of several lines.** In a pin's card and a clip's tooltip, `⇧⏎` starts a new line. The arrows go through the lines, `⌥` a word at a time and `⌘` to the ends (Ctrl, Home and End on Windows and Linux), and with `⇧` they select; a double click selects a word, three the line.

**Report a problem.** Report a Problem… in the menu bar, and Report on GitHub… at the end of Help, open the bug form with asides' version and your system filled in.

**Smaller changes.** The buttons of a card and of a clip's tooltip name themselves under it, as the pill's do, from one to the next without a gap. The keys sheet names both clip keys: `K` for the whole screen, `⌘K` for a pin's area. On Windows and Linux, `Ctrl+←` in a field moves a word instead of going to the start.
